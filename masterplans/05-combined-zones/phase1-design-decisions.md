## Resolved scope controls

### D01 — current-world delivery

The user's clarification and Masterplan 05 agree: the deliverable is the Masterplan 04 composition placed into the current map. Native coordinates in Masterplans 01–04 remain architectural inputs rather than executable setout. The deterministic Phase 1 coordinate transform is suitable for offline fit and ownership coordination; it still has `worldEditAuthorized: false`.

### D03 — omit L2

The proposed MainStreet East L2 study point `(305,80)` is water and no matching cataloged gate exists. The fail-closed choice is to omit L2. No route, owner set, compiler, or release package may target it, and no substitute coordinate may be invented. A future L2 requires a fresh dry survey and its own ownership, structural, source, and release evidence.

### D04 — preserve rail before building rail

The C1 north-flank rail reservation remains 13 blocks wide, from offsets `-30…-18`, with study centerline offsets at `-28` and `-24`. It stays empty during early highway work. Crossings must clear-span it and preserve vertical clearance from the accepted rail profile. This staging choice authorizes neither the highway nor the railway.
