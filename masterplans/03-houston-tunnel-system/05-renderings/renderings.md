# Houston Tunnel System — Renderings Catalog

**Build:** 03 — Houston Tunnel System (Minecraft replica)
**Phase:** 05 — Renderings
**Author role:** Visual Renderer
**Date:** 2026
**Status:** Concept visualizations for the master plan PDF

> The renderings in this catalog are **concept visualizations of the Minecraft build as designed** — Minecraft's blocky voxel geometry rendered to look cinematic, with the documented palette (white/cream/beige tunnel base, fluorescent cool white, tenant brand colors, wayfinding blue). They are not literal photographs; they are what the build is *supposed to look like* once placed.

## Production notes

- All renderings use the documented palette: white/cream/beige tunnel base, fluorescent cool white, tenant brand colors, wayfinding blue. No greys beyond the structural-shell range.
- Aspect ratios per design plan: 16:9 for wide shots and cutaways; 4:3 for detail/interior shots; 1:1 for close-ups (T-marker, plaques).
- The "civilian, climate-controlled, retail-flavored" feel is the dominant signal in every tunnel/food-court shot.
- The "above-ground city" is rendered as a real downtown — not a generic backdrop, but a Houston-vocabulary CBD.
- The "almost-secret" entrance paradox is the key compositional choice in the Wells Fargo and T-marker shots.
