# Masterplan 04 — Combined Complex

Status: **NORMALIZED ARCHITECTURAL COMPOSITION — CURRENT-WORLD PLACEMENT OWNED BY MASTERPLAN 05 — NO WORLD EDITS AUTHORIZED**

Masterplan 04 combines the three child architectural programs into one normalized design:

1. `01-cheyenne-mountain-complex` owns Cheyenne's internal architectural program.
2. `02-subtropolis` owns SubTropolis's internal architectural program.
3. `03-houston-tunnel-system` owns Houston's internal architectural program.
4. `04-combined-complex` owns their normalized composition, inter-site sequence, and local anchors.
5. `05-combined-zones` places that normalized composition into the accepted current-world map and owns the world transform, terrain constraints, external interfaces, additive zones, and delivery gates.

Start with [AUTHORITY.md](AUTHORITY.md) and [authority-reconciliation.json](authority-reconciliation.json). They define the only valid way to interpret this directory for current-world planning.

The controlling Masterplan 04 local-geometry sources are:

- [02-design/site-coordinates.json](02-design/site-coordinates.json)
- [04-contractor/contractor-brief.json](04-contractor/contractor-brief.json)
- [build-info.json](build-info.json)

Those files use normalized Masterplan 04 coordinates. They are not live-world coordinates and are not executable construction instructions. Current-world coordinates come only from [Masterplan 05](../05-combined-zones/README.md).

The `map-integration` research, design, contractor, rendering, and report artifacts are historical placement studies. Their separate-world portal, duplicate schematic Old Town, starter-base coordinates, coastal rail spur, and related data-layer mutations are superseded for current-world placement. `overhead-map-same-world.png` is concept art from that retired scheme, not measured geometry.

The current plan to develop is [Masterplan 05's MASTERPLAN.md](../05-combined-zones/MASTERPLAN.md), beginning with its still-pending Phase 1 detailed-design and interface gates. Phase 0 approved the revised site for detailed design only. No file in Masterplans 01–05 authorizes a world edit.
