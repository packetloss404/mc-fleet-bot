# SubTropolis — Visual Asset Catalog

**Project:** Minecraft replica of SubTropolis (Hunt Midwest's underground business complex, Kansas City, Missouri)
**Location:** 8300 NE Underground Drive, Kansas City, MO 64161 — 39.161213°N, 94.476242°W
**Compiled by:** Visual Scout for Master Plan 02 — SubTropolis
**Image count:** 45 image files + 1 catalog (this file)
**Total size:** ~26.6 MB

All references saved to `references/` subdirectory. Filenames use a category prefix so a quick directory listing tells you what each image is. Sources and licenses listed per asset.

## 8. Gaps

What the corpus is missing.

1. **True satellite / aerial top-down view of SubTropolis.** The official map (`commons-subtropolis-official-map.jpg`) is a stylized site plan. A literal Google Earth / satellite screenshot at 39.161213°N, 94.476242°W would show the actual surface parking, the building footprints, and the relationship to I-435 and MO-210.
2. **1940s-1960s photos of the active Bethany Falls limestone mine** before the 1964 SubTropolis repurposing. This is the single biggest historical visual gap. The mine produced the limestone for Kansas City's roads and airport tarmacs in the 1940s-60s; photos from the Kansas City Public Library's Missouri Valley Special Collections or the Library of Congress may exist but are not yet in the corpus.
3. **A close-up of the actual downhill ramp going into the mine.** The panorama shows the portal and "WARNING 13'-6" CLEARANCE" but no photo is dedicated to the ramp surface and gradient.
4. **A photo of a SubTropolis data center / server room interior** (specifically the SubTropolis Technology Center operated by Hunt Midwest, or LightEdge at 9050 NE Underground Dr). The available data center images are stock iStock material showing generic server rooms. A real photo of the underground data center pods would be ideal.
5. **An infographic showing the "55 million sq ft" scale** (SubTropolis occupies 1,100 acres, equivalent to ~140 football fields, with 10,000+ limestone pillars, 8.2 miles of roads, 2.1 miles of railroad, 500+ truck docks, 1,600+ parking spaces, 55+ tenants, 2,000+ employees). The official map and the room-and-pillar diagrams give the numbers but a designed infographic would be the Visual Renderer's job.
6. **A SubTropolis site plan / wayfinding map** from the Hunt Midwest brochure. The PDF `subsitemap.pdf` referenced in older versions of huntmidwest.com was 404'd at the time of this scout. The same content is partially preserved in the Location page text on huntmidwest.com/industrial-space-for-lease/subtropolis-location/ (visitor driving directions summarized below).
7. **A high-quality image of a SubTropolis USPS / postal area.** The NALC Postal Record article describes the facility but doesn't include a high-resolution photo of the specific underground area. The Jalopnik article has F-150s but no USPS area specifically.
8. **A "Winn Rock Mine" or other repurposed industrial-mine reference.** The original task brief mentioned this; Mega Cavern is the closest representative in the corpus.
9. **A high-resolution image of the visitor entrance / check-in area.** Hunt Midwest's lobby is glimpsed in the entrance render but no high-quality photo of the actual interior visitor experience exists in the corpus.
10. **A close-up of the Bethany Falls limestone itself** — the actual rock texture. This is the dominant visual material of every interior shot but no dedicated texture / macro shot is in the corpus. The visual renderer can probably extract this from the existing interior shots, but a dedicated texture sample would help.
