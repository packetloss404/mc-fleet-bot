## Primary / official sources

**[1] SubTropolis — Wikipedia**
- URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SubTropolis
- **What it covers**: Definitive encyclopedic summary. History from 1945 mining through 1964 opening, 1985 corporate restructuring, 2001 anthrax scare. 14 M sq ft leasable / 16-ft × 40-ft tunnels / 25-ft square pillars / 65-ft centers / 160 ft deep / 65–70 °F / 10.5 mi of road / 50+ tenants / 1,600 employees. Identifies the "World's Largest Underground Business Complex" as a registered trademark. Lists postal operations, NARA Federal Records Center, EPA Region 7 Training & Logistics Center, Underground Vaults & Storage film archive, LightEdge data center, and confirms 2,000+ employees / 6 M sq ft leased as of 2024. Most-cited single source.

**[2] SubTropolis — The Center for Land Use Interpretation (CLUI)**
- URL: https://clui.org/projects/hollowed-earth/underground-storage-and-business-parks-former-limestone-mines/subtropolis
- **What it covers**: Independent, non-commercial description from the Center for Land Use Interpretation. Confirms "largest underground business park in the US." Lists Underground Vaults & Storage, Hallmark Cards, US Postal Service, the SubTropolis Technology Center, vehicle auction and storage yards, trucking terminals, and Worlds of Fun as surface uses. Authoritative third-party verification of scale and tenant mix.

**[3] SubTropolis underground business complex — Hunt Midwest (official site)**
- URL: https://huntmidwest.com/expertise/subtropolis/
- **What it covers**: Hunt Midwest's own marketing. 14 M sq ft facility, 10 M sq ft developed since 1964, 270-million-year-old limestone, room-and-pillar method, 25-ft square pillars, "World's Largest Underground Business Complex"® (registered). 55+ international, national, regional, local companies; 2,500+ tenant employees. Lists current available warehouse spaces (Buildings 86A2, 92A, 73A) with clear heights and dock counts. Sustainability claim: "up to 90 % savings in total energy costs."

**[4] Subtropolis Kansas City, Missouri — ULI Case Studies (Urban Land Institute, 1998)**
- URL: https://casestudies.uli.org/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/C028009.pdf
- **What it covers**: Authoritative 1998 ULI development case study. Confirms 1,100-acre underground footprint, 4.5 M sq ft leasable (as of 1998), 100–150 ft depth, 2,500 surface acres, **65-ft-thick wall** between mining and developed space, Worlds of Fun / Oceans of Fun drawing 1.5 M annual visitors. Typical tenant sizes: 1,200 sq ft office, 60,000 sq ft warehouse/industrial. Includes the **street-level access through entrances in the sides of the hills** — the foundational description of how the mine is reached. Author of the "the limestone shelf begins 50 to 100 feet beneath the surface soil" line.

**[5] Welcome to SubTropolis, a Giant Underground Industrial Park and Road Network Built in an Old Mine — The Drive (Peter Holderith, Dec 25, 2020)**
- URL: https://www.thedrive.com/news/38396/welcome-to-subtropolis-a-giant-underground-industrial-park-and-road-network-built-in-an-old-mine
- **What it covers**: Aimed at car enthusiasts. The 1,100-acre / 1.7 sq mi figure, 16-ft × 40-ft tunnels / 25-ft pillars, 7+ miles of paved roads, several miles of rail, 1,600 employees, $2.25/sq ft rent, 68 °F constant temperature. Notable for the "open to the public — anyone can drive into one entrance of the mine and out another" quote. Names USPS, National Archives and Records Association, film archive (Wizard of Oz, Gone with the Wind), Ford vehicle storage. Drives the "It's basically a big underground city" framing.

**[6] SubTropolis, U.S.A. — Steve Nadis, The Atlantic (May 2010)**
- URL: https://www.theatlantic.com/magazine/archive/2010/05/subtropolis-usa/308033/
- **What it covers**: The foundational long-form magazine feature on SubTropolis. Origin of the "streets are named for geologic layers of limestone or shale" claim. Documents the "Asian Wall Street Journal 'Mole People of Kansas City' 1998" framing. Notes AM-FM radio doesn't reach, truck idling prohibited. Confirms 55 tenants by 2010. Cites Ford's early 1970s 25-acre lease for vehicle storage. The size comparison used is "the size of 140 football fields." Anchor source for cultural context.

**[7] The United States transformed a 270-million-year-old limestone mine into a 1.3 million square meter underground business complex, opening 21 kilometers of streets… — CPG Click Óleo e Gás (July 2026)**
- URL: https://en.clickpetroleoegas.com.br/the-united-states-transformed-a-270-million-year-old-limestone-mine-into-a-13-million-square-meter-underground-business-complex-opening-21-k-ctl01/
- **What it covers**: 2026 engineering trade press summary. Confirms 14 M sq ft leasable, 21 km (13 mi) of streets (likely a high-end number counting all drivable interior road), 55+ companies, 2,500 workers, public archives, postal operations, distribution centers up to 49 m below surface. Notes 2010 figure of 90 % of world's subsurface office space in KC. Independent source for the 2,500-worker current figure.

**[8] SubTropolis TripAdvisor listing (operated by Hunt Midwest)**
- URL: https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g44535-d9597199-Reviews-SubTropolis-Kansas_City_Missouri.html
- **What it covers**: Hunt Midwest's most detailed public spec sheet. **> 6,500,000 sq ft leasable; > 7,500,000 sq ft for expansion; 8.2 mi of lighted, wide, paved roads; 2.1 mi of railroad track; > 500 truck dock locations; served by > 300 truck lines; 55+ companies; > 2,000 employees; > 1,600 parking spaces; > 10,000 limestone pillars; fire sprinklers; limestone compressive strength 18,000–24,000 psi (≈ 6× concrete); > 55,000,000 sq ft total**. Hours of operation (Mon–Fri 6 AM–6 PM). Address 8300 NE Underground Dr, KC MO 64161-9736. Best single source for current operational numbers.

**[9] Data Center (STC) — Hunt Midwest**
- URL: https://huntmidwest.com/expertise/subtropolis/data-center-stc/
- **What it covers**: SubTropolis Technology Center spec sheet. AT&T, Spectrum Enterprise, and Lumen Tier 1 providers. Dark-fiber to 1102 Grand (KC carrier hotel). Biometric hand readers. 24/7 armed security with video surveillance. ENERGY STAR 100 certified. Power, cooling, security, and connectivity specs. Used as a foundation for §6.3 of the report.

**[10] Hunt Midwest properties listings (Building 86A2, 92A, 73A)**
- URL: https://huntmidwest.com/properties/ (and per-building URLs)
- **What it covers**: Current for-lease warehouse specifications. Building 86A2: 108,000 SF, 17 ft clear height, 10 dock doors. Building 92A: 62,200 SF, 17 ft, 14 dock doors. Building 73A: 199,405 SF, 14 ft, 20 dock doors. Confirms the "narrow but tall" vs "wide but short" variability across the complex.

**[11] Contact — Hunt Midwest**
- URL: https://huntmidwest.com/contact/ and https://huntmidwest.com/expertise/subtropolis/contact/
- **What it covers**: Authoritative confirmation of address, phone, and email. SubTropolis: 8300 NE Underground Drive, Kansas City, MO 64161, 816-455-2500, subtropolis@huntmidwest.com. Crossroads office: 1881 Main Street, Suite 200, KC MO 64108.

**[12] National Archives and Records Administration Leases Space in Hunt Midwest SubTropolis (press release) — GlobeNewswire / Hunt Midwest (June 1, 2012)**
- URL: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2012/06/01/478234/20688/en/National-Archives-and-Records-Administration-Leases-Space-in-Hunt-Midwest-SubTropolis.html
- **What it covers**: Most specific public source for federal tenant footprints. NARA: 102,000 sq ft initial lease, options to 372,000 sq ft. USPS Stamp Distribution Center: 217,114 sq ft. USPS Stamp Fulfillment Center: 311,600 sq ft. EPA: 43,200 sq ft. Total federal footprint "nearly one million sq ft." ENERGY STAR 100 (2012). Foreign-trade zone ("largest under one roof"). First project in newest phase north of Derrough Drive; access from Parvin Road. Architects Hoefer-Wysocki; general contractor Valor. LightEdge Solutions $58 M / 21 jobs (April 2013). FoodServiceWarehouse.com $1.75 M / 40 jobs (July 2014). Anchor source for §6.1.

**[13] SubTropolis Mining Map PDF — Hunt Midwest**
- URL: https://huntmidwest.com/pdfs/subsitemap.pdf
- **What it covers**: Sub-site map. "Hunt Midwest SubTropolis | 8300 NE Underground Drive | Kansas City, MO 64161 | 816-455-2500 | Fax 816-455-4462 | SubTropolis.com" — confirms the address used in 2015 by Hunt Midwest itself. Useful for verifying the contact footprint.

## Secondary / news / industry sources

**[14] A run in the underground world of Chiefs founder Lamar Hunt — Axios Local Kansas City (Feb 3, 2026)**
- URL: https://www.axios.com/local/kansas-city/2026/02/03/subtropolis-underground-tunnel-chiefs-founder-lamar-hunt
- **What it covers**: 2026 article on the annual "Groundhog Run" charity race inside the mine. "Enormous 25-foot-wide stone columns support the ceiling, each marked with a number. 'Street' names feature Hushpuckney, named after a local rock type. The speed limit is set at 15 mph." Covers 9 M+ sq ft of developed area, Hunt family legacy, the run's 1982 origin as an Ability KC fundraiser. Independent 2026 confirmation of "anyone can drive in" and pillar numbering.

**[15] Catch SubTropolis, Hunt Midwest in ESPN original 'The Kingdom' — Hunt Midwest news (Sept 2025)**
- URL: https://huntmidwest.com/2025/09/catch-subtropolis-hunt-midwest-in-espn-original-the-kingdom/
- **What it covers**: Press release on the ESPN *The Kingdom* documentary about Lamar Hunt. Confirms SubTropolis as "perhaps Hunt's most unique business idea: an ambitious project to reclaim lateral limestone mining space for millions of square feet of warehouse and distribution buildings. The first tenants included Ford Motor Company, Pillsbury, and Russell Stover."

**[16] Looking for KC industrial space? Try underground — Missouri Department of Economic Development (govdelivery)**
- URL: https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/MODED/bulletins/e0655d
- **What it covers**: Missouri state economic-development press release. 8.2 mi of paved roads, 2.1 mi of railroad, > 500 truck docks, 1,600 parking spaces, 50 M sq ft total, 6 M sq ft leasable, 55 companies, 1,600 employees. Confirms LightEdge ($58 M, 21 jobs, April 2013) and FoodServiceWarehouse.com ($1.75 M, 40 jobs, July 2014) investments. Independent state-government confirmation of the 2013–2014 build-out.

**[17] ENR — Contractor Extracts a Profit From SubTropolis Underground Business**
- URL: https://www.enr.com/articles/261-contractor-extracts-a-profit-from-subtropolis-underground-business
- **What it covers**: Engineering News-Record trade article on Meyer Brothers Building Co. working on a 90,000 sq ft expansion of the NARA storage facility. Confirms "while working 150 ft below the surface in low-lighting conditions has its challenges." The most authoritative construction-trade-press treatment of the active build-out. Notes "the mid-1940s mine, which was excavated through a 270-million-year-old limestone deposit using the room-and-pillar method, continued to operate through the end of the last century."

**[18] Kansas City underground: How to explore the hidden locations beneath our streets — KCUR (Emily Standlee, July 2, 2022)**
- URL: https://www.kcur.org/arts-life/2022-07-02/kansas-city-underground-how-to-explore-the-hidden-locations-beneath-our-streets
- **What it covers**: KCUR (Kansas City public radio, NPR member) feature. "SubTropolis is owned by Hunt Midwest — yes, the same Hunt family that owns the Kansas City Chiefs — and is 'wide enough to hold 42 Arrowhead Stadiums.' There's even a train track winding through the colossal complex, and many, many exit signs." Confirms the drive-through public access. Good Kansas City cultural context.

**[19] SubTropolis: The Massive Business Complex Buried Under Kansas City — OpEx Society (republishing Bloomberg)**
- URL: https://opexsociety.org/thought-food/subtropolis-business-complex-kansas-city/
- **What it covers**: Reposting of the original 2015 Bloomberg piece by Alan Levin. "A treasure trove of business activity housed in a mine the size of 140 football fields … More than 1,000 people now spend their workdays in SubTropolis." Cites the film studios (Wizard of Oz, Gone with the Wind) archive claim. Independent source for the "Bloomberg origin" of many SubTropolis facts.

**[20] SubTropolis: The Hidden Underground City Beneath Kansas City — KC Yesterday (Joe Tinker, Aug 2024)**
- URL: https://kcyesterday.com/articles/subtropolis
- **What it covers**: Local Kansas City history blog. 6 M sq ft leasable, 270-million-year-old limestone. Re-tells the Lamar Hunt story and gives good local KC context. Echoes the "25-ft square pillars on 65-ft centers and 40 ft apart" specs. Less detailed than Wikipedia or the ULI study but useful for cultural color.

**[21] Historical Building Highlight: SubTropolis — Economic Development Corporation of Kansas City (EDCKC)**
- URL: https://edckc.com/subtropolis/
- **What it covers**: Local EDC promotional article. Confirms 50+ tenants, "housed directly under Worlds of Fun and Oceans of Fun," 270 Ma limestone, 25-ft square pillars on 65-ft centers, 40 ft apart. Independent confirmation of the geological / structural specs from a non-Hunt source.

**[22] The Story of SubTropolis & KC's Limestone Caves — KC Yesterday**
- URL: https://web.archive.org/web/20250813142238/https://kcyesterday.com/articles/subtropolis

**[23] Inside the hidden 'city' where 1,600 people go to work each day — Metro (UK) (Caroline Westbrook, March 31, 2024)**
- URL: https://metro.co.uk/2024/03/31/inside-hidden-city-1-600-people-go-work-day-20548088/
