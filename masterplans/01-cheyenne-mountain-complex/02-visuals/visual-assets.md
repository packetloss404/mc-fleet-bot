# Cheyenne Mountain Complex — Visual Asset Catalog

**Source folder:** `02-visuals/references/`
**Total images:** 79 (verified non-empty, non-HTML)
**Total size:** ~51 MB
**Categories:** 7
**Compiled:** 2026-08-02

This catalog is the visual intake for the Minecraft replica build of the Cheyenne Mountain Complex (NORAD's Cold War bunker in Colorado Springs, CO). It is organized to feed the downstream Visual Renderer and Architect. License and source are noted per asset; renderers should prefer the public-domain / DoD files first.

---

## Source Hierarchy (preferred, in order)

1. **US DoD public domain** — NORAD Newsroom, DVIDSHUB, Airman Magazine, US National Archives. Free for any use with photo credit. *Preferred for the master plan.*
2. **Wikipedia / Wikimedia Commons** — Public Domain (PD-USGov) for the original DoD images re-hosted there. CC-BY-SA for the Wikipedia article itself.
3. **Open journalism (CNET, Flashbak)** — historical reproduction of public-domain NORAD imagery; safe to reference as a source.
4. **Stock / Alamy** — visible watermark; **do not embed in the master plan** without licensing. Listed only for design reference.
5. **Minecraft build showcase thumbnails** (YouTube, PlanetMinecraft) — used only for stylistic inspiration; do not embed in the final document.
