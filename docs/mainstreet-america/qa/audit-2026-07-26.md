**Survey date:** 2026-07-26 UTC

## Initial survey finding

The campus is substantially present but reads as an unfinished construction site, not
an upscale residential attraction. The main shells and paved areas survived. The weak
result comes from missing civic infrastructure, repetitive minimally furnished
interiors, blank façades, incomplete service buildings, abandoned roof operations,
and a mostly empty earth-covered east complex. None of the campus assets were present
in `town.db`, `/api/builds`, `/api/markers`, `/api/zones`, or `/api/routes` at survey
time, so there was no durable asset inventory against which dropped work could be
detected.

The remainder of this document preserves that initial evidence and the repair order it
produced. The execution addendum near the end records what was subsequently repaired
and re-verified from a refreshed snapshot on the same date.

## Confirmed defects

### Critical

1. **Unsafe sealed vertical access in the east complex.** The shaft at approximately
   x `199..201`, z `152..155` is air from y `55..101` but has no traversable system.
   Access and fall protection must be installed before cosmetic fit-out.
2. **No authoritative asset inventory existed.** MainStreet America had no project
   features or scan history in the runtime stores, making abandoned and damaged work
   indistinguishable from intended geometry. `WorldFeatureStore` and its API close the
   software gap; `integration/world-features.json` is the reviewed first inventory.

### High

1. **Three abandoned roof jobs:** H03, H04, and H08 contain large open frames outside
   the intended roofs. They are visible in close perspective and their material
   families differ from or extend beyond the canonical roof volumes. H04 also has a
   suspended center span at y84..93; a four-strip check alone is insufficient.
2. **Road/grounds are unfinished:** the smooth-stone strip has no continuous curb and
   sidewalk system, driveway hierarchy, lamp rhythm, or coherent planting. Raw geology
   and abrupt grade faces remain beside developed lots.
3. **Guest Center quality is far below the requested anchor building:** it is a
   145-by-76 rectangular mass with repetitive small windows, blank wall runs, weak
   entrance order, and no convincing mansion-scale cornice, clerestory, glazing, or
   arrival composition.
4. **Service buildings are shells:** the warehouse lacks storage/loading equipment and
   the cooking school lacks a functional teaching kitchen and retail display plan.
5. **Homes do not read as distinct luxury models:** most side/rear elevations are
   blank, interiors repeat the same minimal furniture kit, circulation is not clearly
   authored per plan, and landscape/drive/porch transitions are missing.

### Medium

1. The parking field has the correct gross scale but not a finished luxury-campus
   pedestrian and planting system.
2. The east complex contains large blank corridors and rooms, an empty lantern-lit
   hangar, and a green-floor arena with no resolved purpose or equipment.
3. The campus lacks enough secondary buildings and landscape rooms to make the long
   road feel like a complete neighborhood.
4. The planned white picket boundary requires a terrain-following, collision-safe
   placement pass because the east edge and water/vegetation conditions make a fixed-Y
   fill unsafe.

## Incomplete-work determination

The roof frames over H03, H04, and H08 are confirmed abandoned operations, not
intentional architecture. In contrast, the following are ratified as-built decisions

## Audit acceptance rule

No feature is promoted to `complete` merely because a build command returned
success. Completion requires a refreshed region snapshot, the relevant repeatable
checks, a visual review from street and rear elevations, and a recorded observation
in the world map database.
