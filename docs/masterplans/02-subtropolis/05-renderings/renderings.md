# SubTropolis — Visual Renderings Catalog

> **Visualization package for the SubTropolis Minecraft replica build.**
> Produced by the Visual Renderer for embedding in the master plan PDF.
> 20 renderings total: 15 required + 5 stretch goals.
> All files saved to `modules/`. Aspect ratios: 16:9 (wide exteriors and grids), 4:3 (interior detail shots), 1:1 (close-ups and sign details).
> Style: cinematic concept art with Minecraft blocky geometry rendered photorealistically; 1970s industrial beige palette per the culture-architecture analysis.

**Caption conventions (recommended):**
- Each rendering should be captioned with: the rendering ID, the source rendering name (e.g., "Hushpuckney Avenue"), and a 1–2 sentence pull quote from the culture-architecture analysis that frames the visual.
- For renderings that depict `[X]` (project fictions) — notably 13 (combined overview), 17 (second exit portal on perpendicular face), 18–20 (atmospheric details) — the caption should note "designer interpretation" or "atmospheric detail" so a purist reader knows the boundary.

**Reuse guidance:**
- 03 (Pillar grid reveal) is the most reusable — it can also serve as the section opener for §6 (Tenants) and as a thumbnail on the report's "key takeaways" page.
- 14 (Cutaway diagram) is the only cross-section image; it should be large (full page) and labeled.
- 12 (Hunt Midwest marquee) is the only "marketing" image; it is the visual hook for the trademarked slogan and should be the cover for §8 (Public Facts vs. Myth).
