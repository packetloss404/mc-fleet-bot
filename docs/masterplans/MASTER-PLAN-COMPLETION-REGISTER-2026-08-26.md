## Non-negotiable completion rule

The Master Plan is **not complete** while any required row below is `NOT SET
OUT`, `SURVEY/DESIGN REQUIRED`, `SAFETY HELD`, or `PARTIAL`. A release can be
as-built and still leave its parent programme incomplete. Likewise, a sealed,
deferred, catalogued, map-published, or MVP-accepted scope is not complete.

Before a row may move to **BUILT + FUNCTIONALLY VERIFIED**, its package must
have a fresh immutable source, exact cells/ownership/interfaces, an exact
forward and inverse, container/protected-core and live-entity clearance,
strict-noop parser checks, journaled execution, an immutable post snapshot,
rollback-poststate proof, and the route/mechanism QA appropriate to that row.

## Governor acceleration authority

Named owners, counterparts, and acceptance signatures are Governor-delegated
internal build roles, not a separate approval queue. Independent disjoint
scopes run in parallel; a local fluid, gravity, egress, or interface hold can
stop only its exact interaction set. Historical holds, duplicate surveys, and
publication work cannot be reintroduced as serial gates. The scoped hard-safety
kernel above remains mandatory for every world mutation. [Directive and exact
limits](GOVERNOR-ACCELERATION-DIRECTIVE-2026-08-26.md).
