Planning rule

Dependencies and exact interfaces

Library / loggia

Preserve RRCH-LIBRARY and RG-LOGGIA envelopes. Define exact transition cells and public-route responsibility.

Garth / pavilion / Guild

Neighboring work may share a civic ensemble, but it remains a distinct scope with explicit surface ownership.

Surface water

Closed enumerated basins only. Require liners, leak/overflow QA, neighbor-fluid census, and two independent public routes.

Restricted underground. Default-deny volume: X −148…−64, Y −64…35, Z −562…−420. The Y 36…43 buffer admits no civilian foundation, room, utility, route, or access penetration. No authorized access point is established.
