# Master Plan cold-read review — 22 August 2026

Status: **PASS WITH EXPLICIT OPEN WORK**

This is a reader-first review of the current Master Plan workspace after the
circulation and building-defect reviews. It tests whether a builder can tell
what is real, what is merely cataloged or historical, what must be repaired,
and what evidence is required before changing the world.

## What this review corrected

1. **Raven Rock is not a current accepted complex.** Earlier prose could read
   its catalog and historical dry T2b evidence as an active complex release.
   The Master Plan, runbook, and report input now say that S1 was rolled back,
   T2B was superseded, and no current complex/tunnel/public-route claim exists.
2. **The Ravensreach Moot spine is now a verified repair, not a future idea.**
   `CIVIC-RRCH-MOOT-UPPER-SPINE` has an execution acceptance record at
   `data/world-review/civic-rrch-moot-upper-spine-live-20260822T180000Z/release-acceptance.json`.
   It proves only the 7-wide × 6-high spine, 1:2 flights, seven-deep landings,
   and the tested bidirectional walk; it does not claim its empty or unsupported
   rooms are finished.
3. **Public circulation no longer means a pathfinder can squeeze through.**
   The runbook now requires `CIRC-QA-CORE-01`, including route class,
   width/headroom, rise/run, landings, doors, lights, fluids, ownership, and
   bidirectional no-jump evidence from one fresh source. One-wide stairs and
   two-block headroom cannot be called primary circulation.
4. **The three known cross-area failures stay visible.** C01 portal→L1,
   MainStreet portal→warehouse, and Observatory gallery→hub remain failed
   bidirectional links. They are next correction packages, not silent defects.
5. **Westlight and Ravensgate are not treated as ready just because they have
   catalog records.** Their current egress, clearance, condition, and interfaces
   must be surveyed before repair work is released.

- No current Raven Rock bunker-complex release is claimed.
- Empty Eight PF08 stairs are mechanically traversable but do not meet the

  a separate current guarded release.

## Review conclusion

The plan is execution-ready as a queue, not “complete” as a world. The next
safe physical decisions are the named circulation/room repair cards in the
runbook, each with its own fresh-source proof. This document should be updated
whenever a repair changes a scope card, route classification, or accepted
world claim.
