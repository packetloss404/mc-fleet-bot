## 1. Decision

Treat Masterplans 01–13 as one town system, but do not flatten their evidence
or authority into one overwrite boundary. Accepted physical evidence controls
built status; catalog records describe durable accepted objects; old design
documents control intent only where a later current-world plan has not
superseded them.

The cross-town railway is a proposed delivery program, not an as-built claim.
The current evidence proves bounded roads and pedestrian components, one
552-cell passive B09 rail, one bounded maintenance shuttle over indices
`9..25`, and sealed Empty Eight future stubs. It does not prove a continuous
cross-town passenger railway, public station network, or whole-corridor
operation.

The controlling subway work sequence is the
[Passenger Railway Programme](PASSENGER-RAILWAY-PROGRAM-2026-08-22.md), with
the [existing-rail inventory](PASSENGER-RAILWAY-INVENTORY-2026-08-22.md),
[network/stop register](2026-08-22-passenger-railway-network-design.md), and
[engineering standard](PASSENGER-RAILWAY-ENGINEERING-STANDARD-2026-08-22.md)
as its supporting records. It converts the actual B09 guideway only through
bounded packages; the restricted shuttle is not public service. Every new or
converted passenger segment has a continuous behind-wall **6×6 service
corridor** with controlled exits, built and QA-passed before powered passenger
trials.

required circulation classes and the ordered correction packages.

has a 7-wide × 6-high envelope, 1:2 flights, seven-deep landings, and a
bidirectional normal-walk QA proof. Clerks Hall remains reachable but empty,
and Attic Store still has no proven standable floor; those rooms remain a
separate architectural package, not a furnishing pass. Conversely, the current
Westlight census proves material fabric only; `CIRC-WL-ARRIVAL-01` and
`CIRC-WL-ARRIVAL-ROAD-01` must survey route/egress geometry before any repair
release is proposed.

## 7. Hard execution boundaries

For every physical package:

1. capture a fresh immutable same-invocation source;
2. use complete canonical block states and exact source guards;
3. prove one owner per cell and every shared interface;
4. exclude or exactly migrate entities, containers, block entities,
   mechanisms, fluids, gravity dependencies, utilities, and protected assets;
5. generate exact forward and inverse operations;
6. pass strict-noop parser checks and complete source preflight;
7. execute journaled and fail closed;
8. capture a fresh post, run exact/function/route QA, and prove rollback.

Unknown state blocks only its exact package and dependent seam. It does not
stop unrelated work. Conversely, a bounded package never authorizes a whole
route, public/passenger service, accessibility, hydraulic capacity, utilities,
or external opening.

Database import, map generation, media capture, and publication follow
physical acceptance. They cannot manufacture it.
