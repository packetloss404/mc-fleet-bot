# MP01 Battle Cab staged sequence 01

**Status:** design/ownership sequence only. No Battle Cab cells are built or
commissioned by this document.

## Why it is separate

The completed 15-bay array uses its preserved x-direction inspection corridors,
the central cross-corridor, and the southern inter-row lane for restricted
circulation. Converting one of those cells into a command room would regress
the route proof. The Battle Cab therefore needs a separately surveyed extension
or a reviewed conversion of a specific service bay with a replacement route;
neither is assumed here.

## First exact candidate-envelope survey

The broad west survey rejected `x=1991..1996`: it contains existing air and
polished-diorite fabric and is therefore not an unowned carve target. The
adjacent candidate `x=1997..2007`, `y=164..183`, `z=-878..-861` is **3,960 /
3,960 minecraft:stone** in the immutable post-state used for this design. It
is west of the array and does not consume the x-direction corridors, central
cross-corridor, or southern lane. This is a source-survey finding only; fresh
snapshot, entity/container, route-interface, and exact inversion gates still
apply to BC-S01/BC-S02.

## Required staged release line

1. **BC-S01 — exact candidate-envelope survey.** Fresh immutable source census
   must prove dry, container/entity-free cells plus route ownership and two
   protected approach directions.
2. **BC-S02 — shell and controlled threshold.** Only an independently
   reversible shell, manual boundary, floor, and fixed lighting; post-QA proves
   both protected approaches stay usable.
3. **BC-S03 — passive command-room fit-out.** Non-container console plinths,
   display wall, operator positions, and fixed lighting. No active world-state
   control or public-access claim.
4. **BC-S04 — controls/operations decision.** Any active console, automation,
   redstone state, data device, or public/secure interface needs its own exact
   reviewed migration and operational acceptance. It cannot inherit S01–S03.

The Battle Cab remains an open MP01 completion-register item until all required
physical, route, access-classification, and functional evidence exists.
