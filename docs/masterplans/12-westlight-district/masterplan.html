Risks and holds

Chronology

The district scan is from 2026-07-27; Town Expansion R1 has a later accepted 2026-07-28 terminal snapshot. Reconcile a fresh copied snapshot before claiming current live state.

Scope

The district does not include every venue, road, waterfront or later Westlight-family feature.

Geometry

Functional-zone maps do not necessarily show physical walls. Feature bounds are not automatic safe work volumes.

Preservation

Facade, street or shoreline changes can break interior enclosure, stairs, contents or water containment.

Physical changes are HOLD. Require a fresh immutable snapshot, exact ownership/interface review, guarded forward and rollback plans, route/enclosure criteria and explicit human approval.
