[1] Fort Sasso da Pigna — Wikipedia (English) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Redoubt_(Switzerland) — The English Wikipedia article on the National Redoubt, with a section on Fort Sasso da Pigna. Covers construction (1941–1943), galleries (2,400 m), gun positions (4 × 105 mm), operational period (1943–1999), declassification (2001), and the 2012 opening as the Sasso San Gottardo Museum.

[2] Festung Sasso da Pigna — Wikipedia (German) — https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Festung_Sasso_da_Pigna — More detailed German-language article. Confirms the 2,400 m of galleries, the four 105 mm positions, and the museum's two sectors (Caverna Grande, historical fortress). Names the "Metro del Sasso" inclined lift.

[3] Sasso San Gottardo – Die andere Seite des Gotthards — arttv.ch — https://arttv.ch/mehr/sasso-san-gottardo-die-andere-seite-des-gotthards/ — Swiss cultural TV feature on the museum complex. Notes the 3.2 km of public tunnel, the autonomously operable garrison, and the cultural framing of the site.

[4] Festung San Carlo — Wikipedia (German) — https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Festung_San_Carlo — Definitive source on the San Carlo fortification (A 8390). Confirms 1942 construction, 1998–1999 declassification, 2,060 m above sea level, 4,000 m² area, 2004 opening as "La Claustra" hotel/seminar center, 2010 closure, 2015 reopening by Rainer Geissmann.

[5] Hotel opens beneath mystical mountain — swissinfo.ch — https://www.swissinfo.ch/eng/banking-fintech/hotel-opens-beneath-mystical-mountain/4021610 — Swiss public broadcaster report. Confirms 200 m entrance tunnel, 200-soldier original capacity, CHF 4 million conversion cost, 17 rooms (25 beds), 4-star rating, CHF 325/person/night full board.

[6] ARCH & DESIGN.CLAUSTRA — Jean Odermatt — https://www.jeanodermatt.com/architecture-details — The artist's own site, primary source on the conversion concept. Confirms 4,000 m² underground area, 2,050 m elevation, Airolo municipality, 1998–2004 conversion, prototype Réduit fortification, 2012 sale.

[7] La Claustra: Spirit in der Felskaverne — Handelszeitung — https://www.handelszeitung.ch/unternehmen/la-claustra-spirit-der-felskaverne — Swiss business newspaper. Confirms the Volkart Foundation seed funding, the VBS (Federal Department of Defence) support, 6 conference rooms (25–300 m²), up to 350 participants, CHF 325/person/day.

[8] World's longest railway tunnel opens in Switzerland — China.org.cn (Xinhua) — http://www.china.org.cn/world/2016-06/01/content_38585820.htm — Xinhua news agency. Confirms 57 km length, 2,300 m max rock cover, June 1 2016 opening, CHF 12.2 billion cost, 17 years of construction, Zurich–Lugano 45-min time savings.

[9] Deep inside the world's longest tunnel — CNET (Daniel Terdiman) — https://www.cnet.com/culture/deep-inside-the-worlds-longest-tunnel/ — Eyewitness account of construction visit. Confirms 1 km inside, 800 m below surface, all-flat rail line, opened 2017 (the article was written earlier).

[10] The Helsinki Underground Master Plan (2009) — City of Helsinki, City Planning Department — https://www.hel.fi/hel2/ksv/julkaisut/esitteet/esite_2009-8_en.pdf — The primary source on Helsinki's underground master plan.
