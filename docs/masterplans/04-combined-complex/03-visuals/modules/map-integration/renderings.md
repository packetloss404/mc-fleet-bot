## Production notes

- **Aspect ratios used:** 16:9 for 13 wide shots, 4:3 for 10 detail/interior shots, 1:1 for 1 close-up (the Cute house anchor uses 4:3 for context). Per spec.
- **Minecraft-block aesthetic:** every image shows block-based geometry, pixel-perfect voxel alignment on the schematics, and Minecraft material palette (cobblestone, stone-brick, oak planks, granite, glass-pane, etc.).
- **Color palette:** stone-brick warm-gray for old town + Grand Avenue, polished granite + glass for new city, pink-gray granite for the upper mountain, tan-beige limestone for the lower mountain, green grass + blue sky throughout.
- **Two worlds, one integrated experience:** rendered consistently in 01, 04, 18, 19.
- **No skybridge, no ravine:** every mountain view shows a continuous ridge with a horizontal contact line. The return route is the funicular + summit road (24).
- **The 6 named markers** are visible in 19 and 20; the service-tunnel-contact-crossing marker is at (-40, 200, -360).
- **Funicular return route** is the signature visual of the patched design (24).

## Visual gaps the design plan did not address

1. **Composite terrane plaque at (-40, 200, -360).** The design plan says the plaque is "at the service tunnel contact crossing" but does not specify the plaque's material, dimensions, or sign typography. The design team will need to specify. The plaque is implied (visible) in the contact-crossing icon in `19-visitor-journey-map.png` but no dedicated close-up rendering exists.
2. **The "Mining Area" → "zne_subtropolis_chamber" zone rename** is documented in the data-layer diagram (20) but the *visual* of the new zone (e.g., as it appears in the SubTropolis chamber) is not rendered. The SubTropolis chamber itself is in the 04-masterplan, not in this 5th deliverable.
3. **The funicular counter-weight mechanism** is shown as an inclined rail in 24 but the *full* counter-weight car system (2 cars on a closed loop, with the weight car descending as the passenger car ascends) is not rendered. The 2:1 ratio is implied by the title but not shown mechanically.
4. **The summit road's switchback geometry** is sketched in 24 but the *number* of switchbacks and the *final* descent to the city is not specified in the design plan. The design team will need to confirm the exact routing.
5. **The glass-roof material** of the two Gateway pavilions is rendered as "light gray stained glass" in 02 and 03, but the design plan says it matches between the two pavilions (the *only* material shared). The 02/03 images do not explicitly test the material match; the reader has to compare the two images side by side to see it.
6. **The historical bot avatars (Lilly, Taylor, Marcus, Hazel)** are rendered as small Minecraft player avatars in 17, but the *signage* crediting them on the Gateway station's written book is text-only in the prompt — the design plan does not include a literal "Founded by Lilly, Taylor, Marcus, Hazel, 2026" sign. A small visual that shows the written book open on the lectern would strengthen the historical-credit story.

## Prompts that did not produce good results

None — all 24 prompts produced usable, on-spec images. The synthesis model responded well to:
- Explicit "Minecraft concept visualization" framing
- Specifying *all* materials in the prompt (not just "stone" but "stone-brick", "polished granite", "glass-pane")
- Specifying dimensions in blocks (e.g., "7x7 cross-section", "8 blocks wide", "30x30 plaza")
- Including coordinates when they matter
- Calling out the no-ravine / no-skybridge / horizontal-contact updates explicitly

The most cinematic results were 01 (overview), 04 (portal step-through), 13 (Grand Avenue final 75), and 24 (funicular). The most informational were 19 (visitor journey map) and 20 (data layer).
