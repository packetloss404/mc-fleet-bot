# Combined Complex — Renderings Catalog (No-Ravine Re-Run)

**Project:** mc-fleet-bot Minecraft architecture masterplan
**Location:** Masterplan 04 — Combined Complex
**Author role:** Visual Renderer
**Date:** 2026 (re-run after drop of the V-shaped ravine)
**Status:** Complete catalog of every visualization produced for the **no-ravine** combined complex

> **What changed from the previous run.** The previous renderings were based on a V-shaped ravine splitting the mountain range into a granite north face and a limestone south face, with the composite terrane plaque at the ravine bottom. The new design uses **one continuous mountain** with a **horizontal granite-limestone contact at Y=200**. The composite terrane plaque moves from the (now-deleted) ravine bottom to the **service tunnel contact crossing**, where it is geologically honest (granite plutons push up through limestone — the same geology you see in the Alps, the Appalachians, and the Colorado Front Range). The return route drops the skybridge (which existed only to span the ravine) and becomes **funicular + summit road**. The 7th centerpiece — a summit observation platform at Y=800 — is added. All previous ravine-based renderings have been overwritten with the no-ravine versions.

## What Was Removed vs. the Ravine Design

The previous ravine-based design had a 20f `breccia-display.png` (a bonus close-up of the breccia in a 3×5 display alcove at the ravine bottom). In the no-ravine design, the ravine bottom no longer exists; the breccia is now *inside* the service tunnel at the contact crossing, and the 1-block-wide floor strip (rendering #24) and the contact-crossing alcove (rendering #11) are the canonical breccia views.
