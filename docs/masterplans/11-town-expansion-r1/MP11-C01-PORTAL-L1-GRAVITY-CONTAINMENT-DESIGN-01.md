# MP11 C01 portal-to-L1 gravity containment design 01

**Status:** `HOLD_EXPAND_EXACT_GRAVITY_BOUNDARY_BEFORE_CONTAINMENT_OR_SPINE`  
**Authority:** read-only whole-scope design; it emits no operations or world
mutation authority.

The newest complete source rebinds the known 127-station portal-to-L1 route.
It remains below its required 5×5 controlled envelope and has no accepted
landing sequence. The design therefore inventories gravity before selecting
any widening, excavation, liner, or support cell:

- `1,690` exact gravity cells in the C01 study box;
- `33` connected components;
- `19` finite components and `14` components touching a study boundary;
- zero water/lava/waterlogged cells in the studied volume.
