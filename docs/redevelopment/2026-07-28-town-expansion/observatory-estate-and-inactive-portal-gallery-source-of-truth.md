## Existing condition and critical dependency

The database places `OBS-S01` at `x175..235, y119..136, z137..182` and `APT-S01` at `x178..225, y105..114, z139..180`. Both sit over the old C01 envelope, which reaches `y105`. They cannot be treated as independent buildings while the bunker team removes their support.

Every container and NBT object gets an explicit preserve, move, or retire record.

## Architectural response

Keep the existing white symmetrical Art Deco observatory crown, central copper dome and twin smaller domes. Add lower flanking wings rather than a competing tower:

- west: guest suites, conservatory, art gallery and owner offices;
- east: grand library, music salon, dining room, two kitchens and family rooms;
- south: arcaded arrival court, porte-cochère, sculpture and terraces;
- north: night-sky terrace and a clear approach to the domes;
- outer east: a small five-dish sculptural radio-astronomy garden;
- southwest: pool/spa/gym winter court.

The National Park Service's Vassar Observatory record describes a dome-centered observatory whose telescope rests on massive independent stone piers. That is a useful structural expression: the telescope/dome should read as supported independently from occupied rooms. [NPS, Vassar College Observatory](https://www.nps.gov/places/vassar-college-observatory.htm)

The National Radio Astronomy Observatory presents the VLA as an array of dish antennas accompanied by a visitor center, interpretive exhibits and a signed outdoor walk. The estate's small dish garden uses that public interpretive relationship, not a copy of VLA geometry or operations. [NRAO, Visit the Very Large Array](https://public.nrao.edu/product/vla-sterling-silver-antenna-charm/), [NRAO VLA virtual tour](https://www.aoc.nrao.edu/epo/virtualtour/)

## Expanded shelter

Place the new shelter east of the inventory-dense old storage rooms at `x240..270, y93..104, z170..215`; the broader surveyed volume there has zero fluids and zero block entities. Program it with a safe room, independent air/utility room, stores, sanitation, communications, medical/quiet room and two remote egress routes.

FEMA's public safe-room guidance identifies standby power, protected critical support systems, ventilation, lighting, egress, accessibility, emergency provisions and operations/maintenance as coordinated planning topics. Those categories guide the Minecraft room program; this document does not claim real-world code compliance. [FEMA P-361](https://www.fema.gov/sites/default/files/documents/fema_safe-rooms-for-tornadoes-and-hurricanes_p-361.pdf), [FEMA safe-room design criteria advisory](https://www.fema.gov/sites/default/files/2020-07/safe-rooms-design-criteria_recovery-advisory.pdf)

## Hidden inactive portal gallery

The owner-library's large armillary sphere and constellation bookcase is the fun-to-find architectural trigger. Aligning its celestial model reveals a five-wide descending gallery. This is theatrical discovery, not a description of a real security system.

The underground destination hall has a 21-by-21-block central circulation room, a district compass/floor mosaic, status directory and separately bounded destination rooms for:

- MainStreet America;
- Raven Rock;
- Ravensreach/Ravensgate civic;
- Westlight;
- Panorama Oasis;
- Northeast data campus;
- two scalable spare destinations.

Every room has its own district identity, floor map, return arrow, inactive frame and status sign. Uncommissioned rooms get two sequential metal doors and an `OFFLINE` sign.

No active portal block is allowed. Specifically, the release must prove zero `nether_portal`, `end_portal`, `end_gateway`, active-frame states, or command mechanisms that create them. These are walkable architectural galleries and map rooms only. The portal complex cannot connect to C01, the estate shelter, Raven Rock, Ravensgate, the library–Guild tunnel, natural caves or any other tunnel network.

## Acceptance

The estate passes only when its support is independent of the decommissioned C01 shell, every existing NBT object is accounted for, the shelter's two routes and utilities are commissioned, every destination room is safely walkable, and a block census proves all frames remain inactive.
