# Non-Graphic Adult Interior Design Standard

**Status:** binding design and acceptance standard for the 2026-07-28 redevelopment; it is not evidence of a live build.  
**Applies to:** C01 public adult wing and owner stack, Gilded Raven and its rest suites, Concord adult venues and broadcast rooms, Guest Services adult-only roof/interiors, manager mini-mansions, holdout home, and every other room explicitly assigned an adults-only program.  
**Content boundary:** architecture, furniture, lighting, privacy, hospitality, spa, performance, storage and circulation only. No figures, anatomy, explicit imagery or depicted acts.

## Design intent

An adults-only room may not be an empty box with a sign. It must read as an intentionally planned hospitality or performance space before the program label is visible. The design language comes from three public precedents:

- The Moulin Rouge venue combines a large performance room, food-and-show seating, balconies, controlled sound and light, and many small warm-red table lamps. This supports layered seating, good sightlines, rich ceilings and a visible service/performance relationship rather than rows facing a doorway. Source: <https://www.moulinrouge.fr/en/the-moulin-rouge/the-venue/>.
- The Champagne Lodge describes private ground-level entries, bed-focused suites, fireplaces, whirlpools, steam rooms, walk-in showers, in-room pools, desks and seating. It also describes alternative bed/spa layouts instead of one repeated plan. This supports privacy vestibules, spa zones, differentiated suites and complete furniture groupings. Source: <https://www.thecl.com/>.
- The user's program requires a non-graphic suggestion of red rooms, specialty furniture, beds, lounges and private performance rooms. That suggestion must come from scale, silhouette, palette and adjacency, never explicit imagery.

## Mandatory room anatomy

Every private adults-only room must contain all of the following:

1. A privacy vestibule or offset entry that blocks a direct corridor-to-bed sightline.
2. A clearly modeled bed or upholstered platform with a headboard, foot clearance and two side surfaces.
3. A lounge group containing at least a chaise or sofa, two seats and a table.
4. Dressing space with a screen, wardrobe and closed prop/storage cabinetry.
5. A private wash or cleanup niche with basin, shower or spa fixture as appropriate.
6. Warm dimmable-looking ambient lights plus one brighter task-light group.
7. A decorative focal element: canopy, non-load-bearing frame, suspended lounge seat, fireplace, spa, art wall or small performance platform.
8. Two-block-clear normal walking circulation between entry, bed, lounge, storage and wash areas.
9. An inside-operable exit that requires no key, tool, switch puzzle or special knowledge.
10. Acoustic and visual separation from family rooms, public workspaces, food preparation and required egress.

Closed storage is mandatory. Loose “toy” props may be implied with colored containers, shelves and cabinets, but must not be modeled as explicit objects.

## Public room types

### Main public-view theater

- A real stage and focal wall must be visible from every primary seating group.
- Seating may be tables, banquettes, balconies or shallow tiers, but may not face an entrance door.
- Provide at least two public aisles, a perimeter cross-aisle, accessible-looking open positions and two independent exits.
- Provide lobby, coat/storage, bar, washrooms, dressing rooms, prop/storage, performer route, staff/service route and control position.
- Public and performer circulation must not merge before the stage threshold.

### Small group room

- Five-person target capacity unless an exact schedule says otherwise.
- One focal platform or furniture grouping, five readable seats, privacy vestibule, closed storage and wash niche.
- Must not be a subdivided leftover corridor.

### One-to-one room

- Offset entry, one bed/platform, two-seat lounge, dressing/storage and wash niche.
- Door may not open directly to a main public concourse.
- Multiple rooms need a staffed antechamber and separate service/storage support.

### Adults-only club lounge

- A central bar or hospitality point, multiple seating zones, a quieter salon, visible wayfinding and a deliberate route to private rooms.
- At least three distinct furniture groupings must be visible from the entry.
- Back bar, cold/dry storage, wash station and staff route are part of the room program, not decorative labels.

## Private suite hierarchy

### Manager-house private suite

Minimum readable furnishings: one bed, one lounge/chaise, two additional seats, two side surfaces, one wardrobe, one closed specialty cabinet, one dressing screen, one wash fixture group, one decorative frame or canopy and two lighting groups.

### Tunnel rest suite

Must read like a USA Stadium suite adapted for rest: arrival lounge, refreshment counter, table seating, private themed room, washroom and service closet. It is not a bedroom opening directly off a bare tunnel.

### C01 public private rooms

The doubled public wing must contain at least four one-to-one rooms and at least twelve additional private/themed rooms. Every room receives a unique ID, theme, capacity, entry, furniture census and matched camera candidate. The classified public adult footprint remains at least 5,364 blocks.

### C01 owner stack

The owner club, arrival suite, exact fifteen-suite poly-living level and exact three-bedroom master compound retain their previously frozen counts. Each of the fifteen suites must contain a bedroom, ensuite, rain-shower silhouette, walk-in closet, sitting area, mini-office, front door and rear owner-corridor door. The principal master bedroom may use multiple non-load-bearing furniture frames and a central bed, but remains non-graphic.

## Minecraft furnishing vocabulary

Use the block palette as a coherent visual language, not as a literal real-world equipment specification:

- beds and upholstered platforms: beds, carpet, wool, slabs, stairs and trapdoors;
- sofas and chaises: stair/slab combinations with signs or trapdoors as arms;
- side and dining tables: fences/walls with pressure plates or slabs;
- curtains and privacy screens: banners, wool, trapdoors or offset glazed partitions;
- wardrobes and closed storage: barrels, chests or decorative cabinets only when their block-entity coordinates are explicitly governed;
- warm light: lanterns, candles without uncontrolled fire, redstone lamps behind screens and low-level shroomlights;
- frames/canopies: dark fences, walls, chains and beams that remain decorative and do not obstruct required circulation;
- wash/spa: quartz/calcite/tile, cauldrons, controlled water and glass partitions;
- service bars: counters, back-bar shelving, closed storage, sink/cauldron and staff clearance;
- acoustic separation: double walls, wool-backed panels, offset doors and buffer closets.

Every block entity requires an exact coordinate and NBT policy. Water features require a containment and rollback test. No furnishing may consume a stair landing, lift lobby, airlock, fire/escape route or two-block-clear accessible-looking path.

## Camera and acceptance contract

Each adults-only object must receive:

- one entrance/context image proving privacy and wayfinding;
- one interior image proving bed, lounge, storage, wash and focal furniture;
- for theater/club programs, one audience-to-stage sightline and one backroom/service image;
- for a corrected defect, matched before, defect, fix and second-pass images;
- a content review proving that no figures, anatomy, explicit imagery or depicted acts appear.

Automated and manual acceptance must fail when any of the following is true:

- the room is empty or more than half of its usable floor is an undifferentiated void;
- the only thematic evidence is a sign, red wall or bed;
- the bed is visible directly from a public corridor;
- primary seating faces a doorway or blank wall;
- storage, wash facilities, service support or normal walking clearance is missing;
- adjacent rooms are clones;
- a decorative frame blocks egress;
- a screenshot is linked by filename without exact object/camera geometry;
- a room is marked built in the database before post-state geometry, route and image verification.
