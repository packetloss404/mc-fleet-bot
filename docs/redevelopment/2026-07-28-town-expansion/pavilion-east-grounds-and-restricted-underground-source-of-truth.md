# Pavilion East Grounds and Ravensgate Underground Separation

**Controlling correction:** Ravensgate does not expand. Its recorded district boundary remains `x-148..-64, z-562..-420`. The new work expands the existing civic pavilion ensemble and outdoor grounds above/east of Ravensgate to the flat-land/tree-line transition at `x20`.  
**Release status:** planning only; no live mutation.

## Design decision

The civic composition remains a three-part sequence:

`expanded library → Russian-referenced open civic pavilion/Garth → Great Guild Hall`

The east grounds then continue around the Guild Hall rather than pretending the building is isolated on empty grass. North and south reflecting pools frame the Guild Hall. A Buckingham-Fountain-inspired centerpiece anchors the north garden. A small Lincoln-Memorial-inspired monument terminates the east axis. An allée, flowers, banners, benches, lights and statuary make the tree-line edge deliberate.

The pavilion is not a glass box and is not a literal copy of a Russian monument. It is an original symmetrical masonry-and-timber arcade with a steep metal roof, patterned soffit, central lantern/cupola and integrated sculpture. It remains visually open enough that the library and Guild Hall read as the two bookends.

## Public-source design basis

The National Park Service describes the Lincoln Memorial landscape as an axial composition in which the memorial, reflecting pool, tree-lined walks and reciprocal vistas reinforce one another. That is the useful lesson here: a monument needs a deliberate approach and a long view, not an object dropped into leftover land. [NPS, Views and Vistas of the Lincoln Memorial Cultural Landscape](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/lincoln-memorial-cultural-landscape-views-and-vistas.htm)

NPS also records the memorial's raised approach, plaza, monumental urns and Doric colonnade. The Minecraft monument translates the sequence—pool/approach, steps and ramp, terrace, colonnade, central figure—without copying inscriptions or representing itself as the real memorial. [NPS, Lincoln Memorial Exterior Features](https://www.nps.gov/linc/learn/historyculture/exterior-features.htm)

The Chicago Park District describes Buckingham Fountain as a major sculptural fountain with periodic large water displays and night lighting. Chicago's landmark record identifies it as the centerpiece of a Beaux-Arts garden. Those sources support a strong central basin, cardinal sculpture, a hierarchical jet composition and an evening-light identity. [Chicago Park District](https://www.chicagoparkdistrict.com/parks-facilities/clarence-f-buckingham-memorial-fountain), [City of Chicago Landmark record](https://webapps1.chicago.gov/landmarksweb/web/landmarkdetails.htm?lanId=1257)

For the ceremonial station room, Kyiv's official city guide describes Lukianivska as a three-vault station with pylons, an island platform and multiple underground halls. Its Holosiivska entry describes a low-ceiling hall made visually taller with lighting, a central island platform, white marble, gray granite and green smalt. The tiny Minecraft chamber borrows those public architectural cues while remaining a stationary library room with no rail or network connection. [Kyiv City Guide, Lukianivska](https://guide.kyivcity.gov.ua/en/places/stantsiya-metropolitenu-lukyanivska), [Kyiv City Guide, Holosiivska](https://guide.kyivcity.gov.ua/en/places/stantsiya-metro-holosiyivska)

The Library of Congress describes collection storage as a coordinated program of storage planning, inventory control, preservation, security and accessibility. That supports cataloged closed stacks, controlled service points and conservation support in the two lower archives. [Library of Congress, Collections Management Division](https://www.loc.gov/preservation/about/cmd/cmdindex.html)

## Restricted underground decision

Treat `x-148..-64, y-64..35, z-562..-420` as the frozen Ravensgate restricted underground exclusion. The snapshot census found 36,192 fluid cells, 87,975 air cells, 14,124 gravity-sensitive cells, a spawner and two chests. That is not an invitation to explore. It is evidence that the volume contains caves, water and generated features and should be protected as one restricted object until a dedicated survey proves otherwise.

No civilian/library/Guild/portal tunnel may enter it. `y36..43` is a hard vertical no-penetration buffer. A broader horizontal review buffer applies through `y43`. No access point is authorized in this package. The reserved south-edge access study is sealed and exists only to prevent later teams from improvising an entrance. Any future authorized access gets a named coordinate package and two sequential metal-door vestibules.

The surface grounds may cross above the restricted footprint because their occupied surfaces begin at `y67`, but foundations, basins and utilities still stop above the vertical buffer. Surface expansion does not broaden underground access.

## Isolated library–Guild Hall tunnel

The dry corridor at `x-110..-60, y58..64, z-442..-436` has zero fluids and zero gravity cells. Three dry rooms branch only to its south:

- restricted/challenged-materials archive;
- non-explicit private adult-literature archive;
- Ukrainian-metro-inspired ceremonial station chamber.

This is one isolated building-to-building passage with exactly two parent endpoints. It cannot connect to Raven Rock, C01, natural caves, the owner-estate portal gallery, utilities, or any future tunnel network. The station room is theatrical architecture, not transit.

## Acceptance

No release is acceptable until the Ravensgate exclusion and buffers exist as protected 3D database objects, all surface water bodies pass closed-basin testing, the secret corridor remains network-isolated, and matched sections show the required vertical separation.
