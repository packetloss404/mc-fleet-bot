# Raven Rock (Site R) — Reference Orientation

This folder documents the sources behind a Minecraft reconstruction of the **Raven Rock Mountain
Complex (Site R)** — the U.S. government's underground continuity-of-government / alternate military
command bunker (the "underground Pentagon") near **Blue Ridge Summit, Adams County, Pennsylvania**,
historically tied to Fort Ritchie and Camp David. The full source record is in
[`manifest.yaml`](./manifest.yaml).

This build sits **~20 blocks beneath the "MainStreet America" surface reconstruction** on the same
server. This README exists so that **a visitor is not misled** about what is real history and what is
our invention.

## The honest headline

This is the **mirror image** of the MainStreet America evidence position. There, we had verified
building sizes and a full home roster, and only the *surfaces* were guesswork. **Here it is the
opposite: the *context* is well documented, but the *interior is classified and almost entirely
invented.***

Raven Rock is a **real, well-known public landmark**. Its existence, general location, geology, era,
and mission are solidly on the public record. But **its inside is secret** — there is no public map,
and since **2007 it has been against DoD policy to even make a "photograph, sketch, picture, drawing,
map or graphical representation" of the complex.** So this build is an openly-labeled *imaginative
interpretation* of a public landmark's public-record history — **not** a leaked or accurate map of a
secret facility, and it makes no claim to be one.

## What is genuinely known (verified)

- **It exists and where:** the Raven Rock Mountain Complex, "Site R," inside Raven Rock Mountain near
  **Blue Ridge Summit, Liberty Township, Adams County, PA**, about 6 miles from Camp David.
- **What the mountain is made of:** **greenstone** — a dark green, chlorite-rich *metabasalt* of the
  Catoctin Formation. (Popular articles call it "greenstone granite," but geologically it is **not**
  granite. Build the mountain body as dark greenish hard metabasalt.)
- **When it was built:** land seized in **1951**; blasted out of the mountain **1951–1953**; ~$35
  million by 1954. A half-million cubic yards of rock were secretly hauled away.
- **Its mission:** continuity of government / alternate military command — the **Alternate National
  Military Command Center (ANMCC)**, a backup to the Pentagon's command center (designated 1962).
- **Its most famous use:** **Vice President Cheney was moved there on 9/11 (2001).**
- **Its surface support base:** **Fort Ritchie, MD** (garrison until 1997, then Fort Detrick).

## What is reasonable but not certain (inference)

- **The core design concept — and it's the most build-relevant fact:** freestanding multi-story
  ("three-story") buildings that stand **clear of the cavern walls and rest on giant steel springs**
  (shock isolation), inside blasted caverns, connected by **gently curving tunnels** (the curves blunt
  a blast wave). This is consistently reported and safe to render **in concept** — but the spring
  counts, chamber sizes, and layout are secret.
- **Several tunnel portals** in the hillside (commonly said to be **four**, with about two visible in
  satellite imagery).
- **Self-sufficiency systems** — underground reservoirs, on-site power, ventilation, food reserves —
  are known to exist *in concept*, but their exact number and size are not reliably public.

## What is invented (creative approximation — do not present as fact)

- **The entire interior.** Every room, corridor, staircase, blast door, sign, and furnishing is **our
  invention.** There is no public floor plan. Nothing inside the build is a claimed real layout.
- **The tunnel geometry.** Total length, cross-section, cavern sizes, and how the portals connect are
  all invented.
- **The exact building count, size, and arrangement.** Even the *count* is contested (see below).
- **All coordinates, depths, and dimensions** in the build. The real complex is said to sit ~650 ft
  below the summit; we bury it only **~20 blocks** as a build convention, not a literal depth.

## Things the sources genuinely disagree on

We recorded **both** sides rather than picking one — see the `conflicts` section of the manifest. The
main ones:

| Topic | One source says | Another says |
|---|---|---|
| Rock type | "greenstone granite" | greenstone = *metabasalt*, **not** granite (correct) |
| Building count | **three** buildings (Wikipedia) | **five** three-story buildings (popular sources) |
| Footprint | 280 acres seized | "1,100 Acres" (case caption) / ~650-acre compound |
| Depth | ~650 ft below summit (secondary) | not in primary public record |
| Utilities | "two reservoirs, dual power plants" | existence agreed, counts vary |

For the build, treat the **building count and layout as a design choice**, and render the mountain as
**greenstone/metabasalt**.

## What nobody outside the government can establish

The interior layout, tunnel network geometry, exact building dimensions, current systems and
capacities, personnel, and any defenses. These are **classified**, and we did not seek them. The build
depicts **public history and geology only** — never a live security posture, and never invented detail
dressed up as fact.

Search also surfaced War History Online, National Interest, and an unofficial "whitehouse.gov1.info"
Site-R tour page — the last is a **hobbyist site, not an official White House domain**, and was used
only where a retrieved page corroborated it. No FOIA/GAO PDF was opened directly, and **no source URL
in this manifest is fabricated.**

## How to read the confidence labels

Every finding in `manifest.yaml` carries exactly one of: **verified**, **high-confidence inference**,
**moderate-confidence reconstruction**, or **creative approximation** — never upgraded. Here, that
distribution is deliberately lopsided toward approximation, because the interior is secret.

**Bottom line for a visitor:** the *setting and mission* of this build are real history. The *interior*
is an honest, clearly-labeled work of imagination. Enjoy it as an interpretation of a public landmark —
**not** as a map of a secret one.
